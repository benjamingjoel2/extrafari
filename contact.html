Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

Nine to twelve months ahead for peak season (July to October) and for gorilla permits. Shoulder and green seasons can often be arranged with three to four months' notice.

Is a safari safe for children?

Yes, with the right camps. Many properties welcome children from six and some have no minimum age. We recommend malaria-free reserves in South Africa for younger families, and our Family Safari Kenya is designed around shorter drives and hands-on activities.

What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

A 30% deposit secures your booking with the balance due 60 days before travel. Cancellation terms depend on the camps involved and are set out clearly in your proposal before you commit.

Do I need vaccinations or malaria tablets?

Yellow fever certificates are required for entry to some countries and malaria precautions are advised for most safari areas. We provide a health checklist with every proposal, and you should confirm with a travel clinic before departure.

Can you arrange international flights?

We quote and book all internal flights. For international flights we can recommend routings and connect you with a partner agent, or you are welcome to book your own.
