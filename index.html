The Mara crossings are on

From July to October the herds mass on the Mara River banks. Our guides read the river daily and position your vehicle where the action will be, not where the crowd is.

Gorilla permits sell out early

Permits for Bwindi and Volcanoes are limited and released by date. Plan a year ahead and we will secure yours first, then build the rest of the journey around it.

The Namib after the rains
