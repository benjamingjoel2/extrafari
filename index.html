We are Extrafari

Go further than the guidebook

Extrafari is an owner-run safari operator with teams in Nairobi, Arusha and Cape Town. We plan and operate every trip ourselves, so the person who designs your safari is one call away from the guide who leads it.

Our safaris are private, tailor-made and built around the way you like to travel. With our own guides and vehicles in East Africa and long-standing partner camps across Southern Africa, we take you to the places we know best, in the season that suits them.

#WeAreExtrafari

Our safari destinations

We operate in 8 African countries, with more than 60 hand-picked camps and lodges promising private, wild game viewing, comfort without compromise and a full immersion in nature and local culture.

Kenya

Home of the original East African safari. The Masai Mara delivers the Great Migration river crossings, while private conservancies in Laikipia offer night drives, walking and horseback safaris away from the crowds.

Kenya

Tanzania

The Serengeti's endless plains host the migration for most of the year, from calving in the south to the Grumeti crossings. Add the Ngorongoro Crater, baobab-studded Tarangire and the beaches of Zanzibar.

Tanzania

Botswana

Heed the call of the wild in the Okavango Delta, where the floods arrive in the dry season and draw wildlife to its channels and islands, then meet Africa's largest elephant herds on the Chobe riverfront.

Botswana

Namibia

Africa's most extraordinary landscapes: the towering dunes of Sossusvlei, desert-adapted elephant and rhino in Damaraland, and the floodlit waterholes of Etosha. Rare, remote and made for road journeys.

Namibia

South Africa

Big Five game viewing in the Sabi Sand and Kruger paired with Cape Town, the winelands and the Garden Route. Malaria-free reserves and superb value make it ideal for first safaris and families.

South Africa

Zambia

From the thunder of Victoria Falls to walking safaris in South Luangwa and canoeing the Lower Zambezi past hippo pods, endless adventure awaits in the birthplace of the walking safari.

Zambia

Rwanda

A land of staggering beauty, home to half the world's mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park and a restored savanna teeming with wildlife in Akagera.

Rwanda

Uganda

Gorilla trekking in the Bwindi Impenetrable Forest, chimpanzees in Kibale, tree-climbing lions in Queen Elizabeth National Park and boat safaris on the Kazinga Channel.

Uganda

Travel with purpose

Why travel with Extrafari?

Our camps strike the right balance of comfort and authenticity, and stay true to the soul and spirit of each destination. Private conservancies with caps on guest numbers mean fewer vehicles and people, and more intimate encounters with wildlife in their natural habitat.

Iconic wild destinations

Every Extrafari destination offers a whole new world of environments, habitats, cultures, sights and sounds. From the Mara to the Okavango and the Namib, we take you to the places our guides know best, in the season that suits them.

Discover Africa

Best wildlife experiences

Private conservancies, low vehicle numbers and off-road access put you closer to the action. Whether you are a family, a photographer, a big-cat enthusiast or a birder, we build the safari around what you most want to see.

Africa's iconic wildlife

Guides who live here

Our guides hold the highest available certifications in their countries and many have been with us for more than a decade. They are the reason guests come back, and we hire locally so their knowledge of the wild is the best there is.

Our safari guides

Camps chosen for their guiding

We pick camps and lodges for their position, their guiding and their light footprint, not their thread count. Small, intimate properties spread across vast private areas mean wildlife from your deck as well as from the vehicle.

How we choose camps

Cultural exploration

Each safari honours the cultures and traditions of its area. Village visits are hosted by community partners rather than staged for visitors, and the camps we favour employ and source locally.

Community experiences

Seamless, trusted travel

From the airport meet-and-greet to light-aircraft transfers between camps, our in-country teams handle every step. A 24-hour support line and flying-doctor cover travel with you from start to finish.

Our services

Shared impact

Conservancy fees on our safaris go directly to landowning communities, and every booking supports ranger training and school bursaries near the parks we use. Wild places stay wild when they pay their way.
