About Extrafari

Safaris built by people who live here

Extrafari is an owner-run safari operator with teams in Nairobi, Arusha and Cape Town. We plan and operate every trip ourselves, so the person who designs your safari is one call from the guide who leads it.

Our story

From one Land Cruiser to three countries

Extrafari began with a single vehicle, a guide and a promise: to show guests the Africa its founders grew up with, not the one on the brochure. That meant private vehicles instead of minibuses, camps chosen for their guiding rather than their thread count, and a share of every trip going back to the land and the people who protect it.

Today we operate safaris in eight countries with our own guides and vehicles in Kenya and Tanzania, and long-standing partner camps across Southern Africa. We remain deliberately small. Our planners take on a limited number of trips each season so every itinerary gets the attention it deserves.

What we stand for

Three promises on every safari

Guiding comes first

A great guide turns a game drive into a story. Ours hold the highest available certifications in their countries and many have been with us for more than a decade.

Honest advice

We will tell you when a camp is overpriced, when a month is wrong for the migration, or when two nights is not enough. Our reputation depends on you coming home delighted.

Travel that gives back

We favour community-owned conservancies and camps that employ locally. Every booking contributes to wildlife protection and education projects in the areas you visit.

Conservation & community

Wild places stay wild when they pay their way

Conservancy fees on our safaris go directly to landowning communities in the Mara and Laikipia. We support anti-poaching ranger units, school bursaries near the parks we use, and single-use-plastic-free operations in all our own vehicles and camps.

  • Conservancy partnerships that lease land from Maasai communities
  • Ranger and tracker training scholarships
  • Carbon contributions for all internal flights
  • Annual impact report shared with every guest
