What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Local and session storage are similar stores inside your browser. Both can remember choices or count visits.

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers.

Your consent

The storage listed above is strictly necessary for a feature you choose to use, so it does not need consent. If we ever add cookies or storage that are not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will ask you first and list them here. You can delete cookies and site storage at any time in your browser’s settings.

Other requests your browser makes

Some national park photographs that are not yet stored on this site load from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org), which receives your IP address to deliver the image. No cookies are set through these requests.
