Eight signature itineraries across East and Southern Africa. Each one is a starting point that we tailor to your dates, budget and pace.

Kenya & Tanzania

Great Migration Classic

10 days · Masai Mara · Serengeti · Ngorongoro Crater

The safari that started it all. Three nights on the Mara for river crossings (July to October), four in the central Serengeti and two on the crater rim. Light aircraft transfers keep road time short.

  • Private 4x4 with a Silver-level KPSGA guide
  • Balloon flight over the Mara at dawn
  • Maasai village visit hosted by community partners

Botswana

Okavango & Chobe Waterways

8 days · Okavango Delta · Moremi · Chobe River

A water-and-land safari in one of Africa's last great wildernesses. Fly between intimate camps, explore by mokoro and boat, and finish with the elephant herds of the Chobe riverfront.

  • Three exclusive-use camps, six guests maximum
  • Mokoro, boat and walking safaris included
  • Optional Victoria Falls extension
From $8,400per person sharing Enquire
South Africa

Big Five & Cape Town

7 days · Sabi Sand · Kruger · Cape Town

Four nights in a private Sabi Sand reserve famous for leopard sightings, then three nights in Cape Town with the winelands and the Cape of Good Hope. Malaria-light options available.

  • Open-vehicle night drives and off-road tracking
  • Five-star lodge with private plunge pools
  • Private guided day in the Cape winelands
From $5,200per person sharing Enquire
Uganda & Rwanda

Gorillas & Savanna

9 days · Bwindi · Queen Elizabeth NP · Volcanoes NP

Two gorilla treks, one in Uganda's Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and one in Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park, linked by savanna game drives and a boat safari on the Kazinga Channel.

  • Gorilla permits secured up to a year ahead
  • Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge
  • Moderate fitness required for forest treks
From $7,800per person sharing Enquire
Namibia

Namibia Desert & Etosha

11 days · Sossusvlei · Swakopmund · Damaraland · Etosha

A self-drive or guided road journey through the world's oldest desert: red dunes at sunrise, desert-adapted elephants in Damaraland and floodlit waterholes in Etosha.

  • Guided or fully supported self-drive versions
  • Dark-sky stargazing in the NamibRand reserve
  • Rhino tracking on foot with local conservancy rangers
From $6,100per person sharing Enquire
Zambia

Zambia Walking Safari

9 days · South Luangwa · Lower Zambezi · Victoria Falls

The home of the walking safari. Follow armed scouts and expert guides on foot through South Luangwa, canoe the Lower Zambezi past hippo pods, and end at the thundering Victoria Falls.

  • Bush camps with a maximum of eight guests
  • Canoeing and tiger fishing on the Zambezi
  • Best from June to October in the dry season
From $5,900per person sharing Enquire
Kenya

Family Safari Kenya

8 days · Laikipia · Masai Mara · Nairobi

Built for families with children from six upwards. Family suites, junior ranger programmes, camel rides and horseback safaris in Laikipia, then a classic Mara finale with shorter drives.

  • Child-friendly lodges with pools and family tents
  • Junior ranger tracking and bush skills courses
  • Giraffe Centre and elephant orphanage in Nairobi
From $4,600per person sharing Enquire

Honeymoon Tanzania & Zanzibar

  • Romantic camps with private decks and bush baths
  • Beachfront villa on Zanzibar with private butler
  • Honeymoon touches included at every property
