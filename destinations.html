Destinations

Where we take you

Eight countries, each with its own rhythm. Our planners have travelled every one of them and will tell you honestly which fits your dates and your dream.

Search every national park in Africa

East Africa

Kenya

The original safari country. The Masai Mara delivers the Great Migration river crossings, while private conservancies in Laikipia and the Mara offer night drives, walking and horseback safaris away from the crowds.

  • Best timeJuly to October, January to February
  • Signature sightMara River crossings
  • Great forFirst-timers, families, photographers
  • Pair withTanzania, Diani Beach, Lamu
Kenya safaris
East Africa

Tanzania

The Serengeti's endless plains host the migration for most of the year, from calving season in the south to the Grumeti crossings in the west. Add the Ngorongoro Crater, baobab-studded Tarangire and the beaches of Zanzibar.

  • Best timeJune to October, January to March for calving
  • Signature sightNgorongoro Crater floor
  • Great forHoneymoons, migration, bush-and-beach
  • Pair withZanzibar, Kenya, Kilimanjaro climb
Tanzania safaris
Southern Africa

Botswana

Low-volume, high-value safari at its best. The Okavango Delta floods in the dry season, drawing wildlife to its channels and islands, while Chobe holds Africa's largest elephant population.

  • Best timeMay to October
  • Signature sightMokoro trips through the delta
  • Great forLuxury, seclusion, repeat safari-goers
  • Pair withVictoria Falls, Namibia, Cape Town
Botswana safaris
Southern Africa

South Africa

Big Five game viewing in Sabi Sand and Kruger paired with Cape Town, the winelands and the Garden Route. Excellent roads, malaria-free reserves and superb value make it ideal for first safaris and family trips.

  • Best timeMay to September for game, year-round for the Cape
  • Signature sightLeopards of the Sabi Sand
  • Great forFamilies, food and wine, short trips
  • Pair withBotswana, Victoria Falls, Mozambique
South Africa safaris
Southern Africa

Namibia

A landscape safari: the towering dunes of Sossusvlei, the shipwrecks of the Skeleton Coast, desert-adapted elephants and rhino in Damaraland, and the floodlit waterholes of Etosha. Ideal for road trips and photographers.

  • Best timeMay to October
  • Signature sightSunrise from Dune 45
  • Great forSelf-drive, photography, stargazing
  • Pair withBotswana, Cape Town
Namibia safaris
Southern Africa

Zambia

The birthplace of the walking safari. South Luangwa is leopard country, the Lower Zambezi offers canoeing between elephants, and Victoria Falls is at its most thunderous from February to May.

  • Best timeJune to October
  • Signature sightWalking with armed scouts in South Luangwa
  • Great forAdventure, experienced safari-goers
  • Pair withBotswana, Malawi's Lake Malawi
Zambia safaris
East Africa

Uganda & Rwanda

Home to the world's remaining mountain gorillas. Trek in Bwindi or Volcanoes National Park, track chimpanzees in Kibale, then swap the forest for the savanna of Queen Elizabeth or Akagera.

  • Best timeJune to September, December to February
  • Signature sightAn hour with a gorilla family
  • Great forPrimates, active travellers, bucket lists
  • Pair withKenya, Tanzania
Gorilla safaris

Safari seasons at a glance

Dry seasons concentrate wildlife at water and thin the grass. Green seasons bring baby animals, birds and lower prices. There is no bad month, only different safaris.
